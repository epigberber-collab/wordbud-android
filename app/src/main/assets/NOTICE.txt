The lexicon.tsv.gz asset is an adaptation: simplified Chinese entries,
normalized toneless pinyin (v for ü), first two useful English definitions,
basic candidate ordering. This adapted data remains CC BY-SA 4.0.
Conversion source is included in tools/make_lexicon.py in the source archive.

WordBud / 词芽 is an independent unofficial prototype inspired by the concept
of displaying translations beside input candidates. It does not incorporate
Qingjian code, brand name or logo, and is not affiliated with its maintainers.
